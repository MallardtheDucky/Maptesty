About This Archive

This survey is compiled and maintained under Continuance protocol, cross referencing faction holding disclosures against the Continuance's own institutional memory, a record stretching, in fragments, toward 1806.

Site markers are flagged by confidence: CONFIRMED locations are drawn from verified disclosures, APPROXIMATE locations are drawn from partial or redacted records, and CLASSIFIED markers indicate a site whose true location remains unknown to this archive. Their map position is a placeholder within the stated region only.

Click any country on the survey map to zoom in and reveal its internal states, provinces, or territories. Where the archive's records go deeper, use the level toggle in the regional window to drill further, down to districts and municipalities. Use Dossiers to browse factions directly, Zones to review publicly acknowledged Exclusion Zones, and Timeline to review the historical record of the Rift Era.

Subdivision boundaries beyond national level are retrieved live from geoBoundaries (geoboundaries.org), used under CC BY 4.0, with attribution to the original national sources it aggregates.
